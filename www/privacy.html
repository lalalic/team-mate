Privacy Policy - MSTeams Meeting AI Assistant

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of personal information we receive and collect when you use the "MSTeams Meeting AI Assistant" Chrome extension, as well as how we safeguard your information.

Information We Collect

Meeting captions, uploaded knowledge, settings, and local Premium entitlement are stored in your browser. We do not operate a meeting-content server or collect payment details.

How We Use Information

Captions are sent to your configured AI provider only when you explicitly ask a question. Stripe handles any Premium checkout; MeetMate does not receive card details.

Cookies

We do not use cookies in our Chrome extension.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at ai.qili2.com.
